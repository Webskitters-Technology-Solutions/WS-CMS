# Code Of Conduct

WTS CMS is maintained as a professional and respectful open-source project powered by Webskitters Technology Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about technical risks.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Welcome people with different experience levels.
- Give maintainers enough context to reproduce issues.
- Accept decisions when maintainers explain project scope or priorities.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic, spammy, or disruptive comments.
- Pressuring maintainers to bypass security or quality requirements.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or accounts from project spaces when behavior harms the community or project.

Serious incidents can be reported through the contact process in [`SUPPORT.md`](./SUPPORT.md). Security-sensitive incidents should follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation spaces, and community channels connected to WTS CMS.
